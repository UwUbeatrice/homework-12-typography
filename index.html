The Art of Typography

Typography is more than just choosing fonts. It is the art of making words beautiful, clear and easy to read.

Why Typography Matters

Typography plays an important role in web design. The right font, size and spacing can make a website look more professional and help people understand the information more easily.

Different fonts can create different feelings. Some look modern and simple, while others feel more classic and elegant. Choosing the right typography makes a big difference.

"Typography is the voice of written words." — Design Inspiration

Three Important Principles

01. Font Choice

Choosing suitable fonts helps create a consistent and attractive design.

02. Hierarchy

Different text sizes help readers understand which information is most important.

03. Spacing

Good spacing makes text more comfortable and easier to read.

04. Readability

Clear typography makes websites more user-friendly.
